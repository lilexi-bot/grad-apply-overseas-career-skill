## Principle 1 — 真实感 > 高大上 (Authenticity beats grandiosity)

> 哈佛前招生官曾说："我们想看到申请者如何在矛盾中做选择，而不是标准答案。"

- The PS must convey **authenticity** — choices made *in contradiction*, not textbook answers.
- Showing passion *subtly* is the real craft.
- **How to demonstrate passion without stating it:**
  - Name specific moments of curiosity, confusion, or friction.
  - Show the *evolution* of interest over time.
  - Reference specific faculty work / papers / lab output that genuinely moved or provoked you.

## Principle 2 — 用细节讲故事 (Details beat awards lists)

- A vivid, scene-rich fragment is more powerful than a list of awards.
- To stand out, the PS must have **distinctiveness**.
- **Stand in the admissions officer's shoes:** write something *interesting*, *distinctive*, and *compelling enough to keep reading*.

## Principle 3 — 思想深度决定上限 (Depth of thought sets the ceiling)

- The admissions officer wants a student with **professional self-awareness about the future**.
- Must demonstrate: understanding of the industry + clear, professional plan.
- Requires deep familiarity with: sub-branches / certifications / roles / career paths.

## Principle 4 — 5段式叙事法 (The 5-paragraph narrative arc)

| Para | Function |
|---|---|
| 1. **高光引入** | Open with single most relevant, brightest internship / project. How you encountered the field. |
| 2. **挑战与反思** | Specific challenge → which of your own weaknesses became visible (gaps the target programme can fill). |
| 3. **溯源与证明** | Trace motivation back to earlier academic / campus experience; use achievements to prove long preparation. |
| 4. **完美契合** | **Why School**: to fill the gaps you decided to pursue graduate study, and this programme's XX course / XX professor / XX resources is exactly what you need. |
| 5. **展望未来** | Short-term + long-term career goals; re-emphasise the programme as the bridge from "me" to "a better me". |

## Anti-patterns
- Generic declarations of passion. Violates Principle 1.
- Awards list disguised as paragraphs. Violates Principle 2.
- Vague career goals. Violates Principle 3.
- Flat narrative without tension. Violates Principle 4 (the 挑战与反思 beat is essential for tension).

🎩真实感 > 高大上 哈佛前招生官曾说："我们想看到申请者如何在矛盾中做选择，而不是标准答案。"需要我们在写的时候体现真实感，但是在不可能赤裸裸地陈述"我爱死你们专业了！"的情况下，如何潜移默化表现出申请人Passion去感染招生官，是一个非常考验水平的微妙问题。 . 🎀用细节讲故事 用画面感极强的片段，比罗列奖项更有力。怎样在茫茫文字海洋中吸引到招生官，文书一定要有独特性，所以在写文书的时候可以用细节丰富故事，也会更有真实性。力求站在招生官角度，写出来有趣的、有特色的、能够吸引他们读下去的文书。 . 🔋思想深度决定上限 招生官要的一定是一个对自己的未来有专业认知的学生，所以文书中一定要体现申请人对行业的了解、对自己未来的清晰而专业的规划。要想在这部分脱颖而出，一定要有多商科各个分支行业、各个证书、各个职位等等有充分的积累。 . 📝 文书5段式叙事法 ◾️第1段：高光引入 - 从你最相关、最亮眼的一段实习/项目经历切入，简述你如何因此接触该领域并产生浓厚兴趣。 . ◾️第2段：挑战与反思 - 描述在探索过程中遇到的具体挑战，从而自然引出你意识到自己哪些方面的不足（这里正好对应项目能弥补你的点）。 . ◾️第3段：溯源与证明 - 将你的动机追溯到更早的学术或校园经历，用相关成就证明你一直为此在做准备。 . ◾️第4段：完美契合 - 论证Why School，说明为了弥补不足，你决定深造，而这个项目的XX课程、XX教授的研究、XX资源正是你需要的。 . ◾️第5段：展望未来 - 真诚总结，说明你的短期和长期职业目标，并再次强调这个项目是如何帮你实现从"我"到"更好的我"的关键桥梁。

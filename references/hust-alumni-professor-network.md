## How to use this list

1. **Find alumni in your target direction** — scan the Research Field column for your area.
2. **Cross-reference with CSRankings** — check if the professor is still active (last 3–5 years
   of top-venue publications).
3. **Check current status** — visit the professor's official homepage to confirm: still at the
   same institution? Still accepting students? Recent papers in your sub-area?
4. **Warm outreach** — when emailing, mention the shared HUST background. Alumni are significantly
   more likely to respond to a fellow 华科 student.
5. **面套 opportunity** — if you're doing 暑研 in the same region, plan a visit. In-person meetings
   are dramatically more effective than email.

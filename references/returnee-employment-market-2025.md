## 中文原文（verbatim）

2025海归就业现状大起底📊 刚刷到智联的海归就业报告，看完真的有点感慨，来跟大家唠唠真实情况👇

1️⃣ 回国规模创新高
2025年回国求职的海归人数是2018年的1.51倍，应届海归更是达到2.25倍，近八年新高。大家都在往回跑，竞争真的越来越卷了😮‍💨

2️⃣ 英国仍是最大生源地
英国海归占比33.5%稳居第一，澳洲21.6%紧随其后。但增速上，马来西亚、新加坡的海归人数同比暴涨65%和50.1%，亚洲留学回来的小伙伴越来越多啦～

3️⃣ 硕士占比超八成
80.9%都是硕士学历，本科和博士分别是16%和3.1%。专业上，金融、会计、法学这些传统商科社科还是热门，但计算机、数据科学、AI这些新质生产力相关专业也冲进了TOP20，技术岗越来越香✨

4️⃣ 行业选择有变化
教培、咨询、贸易还是吸纳海归的主力，平均月薪1.2-1.6万。但新质生产力方向的待遇更卷：医药制造17493元，电子/半导体16263元，新能源15933元，跟国际业务相关的投资/融资、电力/水利/热力/燃气更是超1.8万💰

5️⃣ 城市选择在下沉
虽然北京、上海、深圳还是投递量前三，但五线城市海归投递增速30.8%，远超一线的11.3%。大家不再只盯着北上广，新一线和二三线城市的机会也越来越多

## Key Takeaways (English)

1. **Supply surge — competition intensifying.** 2025 returnee job-seekers are 1.51× the 2018 level; fresh-graduate returnees hit 2.25×, an 8-year high. An overseas degree alone no longer differentiates — you need a concrete skill moat.

2. **UK remains #1 source, but Asia is surging.** UK 33.5%, Australia 21.6%. Malaysia & Singapore up 65% / 50.1% YoY — shorter programmes + lower cost are pulling students away from traditional Anglo destinations.

3. **Master's degree is the default.** 80.9% hold a master's; only 3.1% are PhDs. STEM/AI-related fields are now in the top-20 majors alongside traditional business/law — the market rewards technical skills even among returnees.

4. **Salary by industry — "new productive forces" pay premium.**
   | Sector | Avg. monthly salary (CNY) |
   | --- | --- |
   | Education / Consulting / Trade | 12,000–16,000 |
   | New energy | 15,933 |
   | Electronics / Semiconductor | 16,263 |
   | Pharmaceutical manufacturing | 17,493 |
   | Investment / Financing / Utilities | 18,000+ |

5. **City-tier is decentralising.** Tier-1 (Beijing/Shanghai/Shenzhen) still lead in absolute volume, but Tier-5 cities saw 30.8% YoY growth in returnee applications vs. 11.3% in Tier-1. Lower-tier cities are becoming viable career options.

6. **Strategic implication for school/country selection.** When advising students: (a) a master's from the UK/Australia is now the *baseline*, not a differentiator — programme choice and skill stack matter more; (b) emerging destinations (MY/SG) are ROI-positive for cost-conscious families; (c) STEM/AI programmes have a measurable salary premium over pure business degrees in the returnee job market.

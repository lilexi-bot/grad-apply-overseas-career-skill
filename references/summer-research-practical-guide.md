# Summer Research (暑研) Practical Guide — from finding a lab to face-to-face networking

> Source: HUST CS 飞跃手册 2023 (https://gitee.com/zcysky_Shiroha/hust-cs-feiyue-2023).
> This guide covers the full lifecycle: when to start, how to find a lab, what to do during
> the research, and how to leverage 暑研 for PhD admissions. Adapted for CS students but
> applicable to ECE/DS and related fields.
> （暑研实操指南，找实验室/套磁/面套/推荐信/时间线）。

## Why 暑研 matters for PhD admissions

暑研 is nearly indispensable for competitive PhD admissions. Its purposes, in order of importance:
1. **A strong overseas recommendation letter** from a professor who directly supervised you
2. **Face-to-face interviews** with professors at the host institution and nearby schools
3. **Potential research output** (paper under review / in progress by application deadline)
4. **English communication skills** improvement through daily lab interaction

## When to do 暑研

- **Timing:** Summer between junior and senior year (大三暑假). Duration: 3–4 months (July–October).
- **Preparation starts:** Winter of junior year (大三寒假) — start emailing professors.
- **Prerequisites:** Core CS coursework completed; at least one semester of lab research at home institution; English test scores at acceptable level (ideally TOEFL 100+ already).

### Path 2 — Self-套磁 (the standard route)
Email professors directly. Your email should include:
- High GPA, research experience, language scores
- **Self-funded** statement (most critical — professors have limited funding for visiting undergrads)
- Specific research interest aligned with their recent publications

**Key tips:**
- Start from winter break (大三寒假); send emails early morning US time (6–7 AM)
- Reply rate is LOW — this is normal. Persist.
- Groups that accept 暑研 students tend to accept them every year; groups that don't, don't.
- Don't limit to your exact sub-direction; "cast a wide net" across related areas
- Update interested professors periodically with research progress

### Path 3 — Professor referral (lowest risk)
Ask a HUST professor with overseas connections to recommend you to their network. Requirements:
- The professor must know you well (prior research mentor, 实验班导师, etc.)
- You must have a reasonable CV
- Self-funded preferred
- Approach with humility and sufficient notice

## What to do DURING 暑研

### The six priorities (in order)

1. **Secure the recommendation letter.** Before leaving, have a direct conversation with your advisor:
   - Share your application goals and target schools
   - Ask explicitly: "Would you be willing to write me a strong letter?"
   - Clarify the number of letters they'll support (10–15 is standard in the US)
   - If they cap it, accept gracefully — don't push

2. **Do real research + write weekly reports.** Even if progress is slow, a weekly update shows
   diligence and makes it easy for the professor to write your letter later.

3. **面套 (face-to-face visits) to target professors.** This is the highest-ROI activity:
   - Identify 3–5 professors at your host school + nearby schools
   - Email them requesting a brief meeting (prepare a research PPT)
   - Send the email early morning on a workday for maximum visibility
   - After the visit, send a thank-you email within 24 hours
   - Maintain contact through the application season

4. **Retake TOEFL (optional).** If you're in an English-speaking environment, your listening/speaking
   may have improved. US test centres have abundant availability.
   ⚠️ Do NOT leave GRE for 暑研 — it has nothing to do with daily English improvement.

5. **Draft and polish SOP/CV overseas.** Your peers abroad are:
   - Native-level English speakers
   - Experienced with the application process
   - In your field — they understand your research context
   Ask them to review your materials. If available, also ask the host professor and Writing Center.

6. **Gather insider information.** Talk to current applicants and senior students about:
   - Which programmes are unexpectedly competitive
   - Which professors are known to be difficult or supportive
   - Interview styles at specific schools

## 面套 logistics (US-focused)

| Need | Recommended Tool |
| --- | --- |
| Flights | Student Universe (student discounts) |
| Intercity bus | Wanderu (Greyhound, ~$20-30) |
| Rideshare | Lyft (slightly cheaper) / Uber |
| Accommodation | Airbnb (check distance to campus + neighbourhood safety) |

**面套 day-of:**
- Prepare a PPT about your prior research (tailor emphasis per professor)
- Offer to present: "I've prepared a brief overview of my previous work, happy to share if you're interested"
- Accept lab tours graciously; ask questions
- Send thank-you email same day

## Timeline for HUST CS students (ideal)

| When | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Freshman year | Focus on GPA; start TOEFL prep |
| Sophomore year | Enter a lab; begin foundational research |
| Junior fall | Produce research results; finalise TOEFL/GRE |
| Junior winter break | Start 暑研套磁 emails; read professors' pages |
| Junior spring | Continue remote research with target lab; refine CV |
| Junior summer (Jul–Oct) | 暑研 abroad; 面套; draft SOP; retake TOEFL if needed |
| Senior fall | Submit applications; follow up with contacts |

## Anti-patterns

- **游学 / study-abroad tourism programmes:** Zero value for PhD admissions. Avoid.
- **暑期学校 (summer school) for PhD prep:** A professor told the 飞跃手册 author directly: "没用."
  Limited exceptions for specific CS courses, but still far inferior to actual lab research.
- **Waiting for perfect conditions:** Start套磁 even if your CV isn't "ready." The套磁 process itself
  is preparation for PhD applications.
- **Ignoring self-funding reality:** Most professors won't pay for a visiting undergrad. State
  "self-funded" explicitly — it dramatically increases reply rates.

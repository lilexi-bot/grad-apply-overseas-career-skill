## The 7 takeaways

### 1. Wealth freedom is first and foremost **spiritual freedom**
> 财富自由本身首先是一种精神自由

Freedom from having to make *value compromises* for money — compromises that include: time,
attention, emotion, and dignity. The financial outcome is downstream of this mental posture.

### 2. Mental wealth freedom tends to come **first**; financial freedom follows
Many people first reach wealth freedom at the **spiritual level**, entering a positive feedback loop:
- doing what they like, with people they like, in an environment that does not extract the
  compromises above;
- this increases their resistance to external noise, which lets them do more of what they want,
  which makes them work harder;
- having first entered a *wealth-free mental environment*, their financial environment gradually
  improves too; sustained accumulation eventually produces *financial* wealth freedom.

The order matters: **mental → financial**, not the reverse.

### 3. The core reason most people are **hard-working but not wealthy** is **lack of strategy**
> 越穷就越应该制定人生战略，即想清楚自己要做什么事

**Do not confuse tactical diligence with strategic laziness.** The most common failure mode is
working very hard on the wrong question.

### 4. Buying things almost never produces sudden wealth; the best investment for young people is
**investing in yourself**
> 通过买什么东西实现一夜暴富几乎不可能，所以年轻人最值得推荐的投资是"投资自己"

### 5. Most people are living inside an **un-examined social script**
> 为什么要结婚、为什么要买车、有什么要买房子，绝大多数人是不会反思的，蒙着头就像执行程序一样开始执行了。

Most people do not question *why* they should marry, buy a car, buy a house — they execute the
default social program without reflection. Recognising this is itself a form of wealth freedom.

### 6. Dopamine from entertainment activities is **decreasing**
> 娱乐活动分解的多巴胺是递减的。

Biologically, consumption-based happiness has diminishing marginal returns. The implication: a life
optimised for consumption has a hard ceiling.

### 7. Lottery winners rarely stay wealthy
> 有80%的人的财富在中了彩票的40年后回到了现状，无论这些中奖者是20岁、30岁还是40岁中的。

Cited from *American Wealth Weekly*: 80% of lottery winners are back to their original financial
state within 40 years, regardless of age at winning. Windfall without mental framework collapses.

## Anti-patterns this file guards against
- **Tactical diligence masking strategic laziness** — the single most common failure mode.
- **Consumption-based happiness optimisation** — has a hard ceiling (takeaway 6).
- **Windfall without mental framework** — collapses within one generation (takeaway 7).
- **Executing the default social script un-examined** — marrying / buying a car / buying a house
  without asking *why* (takeaway 5).
- **Reversing the order** — trying to reach financial freedom first and hoping mental freedom
  follows (takeaway 2 says the order is the opposite).

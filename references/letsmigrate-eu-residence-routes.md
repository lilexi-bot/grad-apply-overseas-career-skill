### 中国材料准备指南的核心要点

**原则：从目标国清单倒推材料。** 欧洲居留申请在递交表格之前就可能成败已定——难点是材料就绪度。

**七类材料桶（core document buckets）：**
1. Identity：护照、旧护照、照片、居住史
2. Civil status：结婚证、出生证、离婚记录、抚养权证明
3. Police and conduct：无犯罪记录证明
4. Work and income：雇佣合同、雇主信、工资单、银行流水、税务记录
5. Education：学位、成绩单、职业资格、学历认证/可比性证明
6. Business：公司注册、股东记录、报税、客户合同、发票（创业/自雇路线）
7. Housing and insurance：租约、邀请函、住宿证明、医保、旅行保险

**认证与翻译检查：**
- 中国 2023 年加入海牙公约（Apostille Convention）后，很多跨境流程简化，但没有消除所有国别要求——收件机关仍可能要求特定文件时效、翻译类型（certified vs sworn translation）、原件格式、本地申报顺序
- 正确的问题永远是："对这个国家、这条路线，caseworker 接受什么格式？"
- 蓝卡材料：学位和资格证明要早办；数字游民：收入/远程合同/税务/保险最关键；家庭团聚：民事状态文件常因姓名罗马拼法/日期不一致成为最慢环节

**实操五步：**
1. 选定路线，下载当前官方 checklist
2. 标记每份中国出具文件需要 apostille / 领事认证 / 公证 / 翻译中的哪一种
3. 检查无犯罪、银行流水、保险、民事文件的有效期窗口
4. 统一护照、学位、合同、银行、家庭文件上的姓名拼法
5. 原件、扫描件、翻译件、apostille 页、快递回执归入一个档案

## English Reading Guide

LetsMigrate.org is a decision-oriented database of European migration routes, explicitly built with Chinese applicants in mind (China document-prep hub, Blue Card and digital-nomad comparisons "for Chinese skilled workers/applicants"). Its differentiator vs. law-firm blogs and immigrations agencies: intent-based routing (remote worker / job offer / founder / investor / still comparing), programme-level coverage (93+ programmes across 5 route families), and an editorial discipline of timestamps + official-source links + scheduled re-verification.

**How to use it in career/visa planning:**

1. **Route-first, not country-first.** Start from your constraint set (remote income vs. offer vs. operating business vs. capital), then shortlist countries. This avoids the common mistake of fixating on one country and discovering no usable route.
2. **For skilled workers:** the EU Blue Card comparison (26 countries, 50 programmes) gives salary thresholds and settlement timelines — pair with the Germany guide (IT experience channel) when evaluating Europe as a US-visa Plan B.
3. **For founders:** 16 startup/self-employed routes — cross-reference with this repo's founder playbook (France French Tech Talent Passport, Netherlands DAFT-like routes, Estonia e-residency adjacency).
4. **For China-issued documents:** the document-prep hub is the operational checklist — apostille after China's 2023 Hague accession simplified but did not eliminate country-specific legalisation; name romanisation consistency and validity windows are the two silent killers of family applications.
5. **Verify before relying:** check each guide's "Verified / Next review" dates and always click through to the official source; rules (especially golden visas and DNV thresholds) change frequently — Portugal's ended golden visa for real estate is the canonical example, and the site explicitly tracks "ended routes."

## English Key Takeaways

1. **Five route families:** digital nomad (15), skilled work/Blue Card (50 programmes/26 countries), startup/self-employed (16), investment (10, with ended-route tracking), alternatives/passive residence (2).
2. **Deepest country coverage:** Malta, Portugal, Italy, Cyprus, Greece, Hungary, Latvia (14–18 guides each); Germany guide notably includes 2026 Blue Card thresholds and the IT experience path.
3. **China-specific value:** dedicated Chinese-applicant document hub — 7 document buckets, apostille/translation checks, 5-step preparation sequence, name-consistency warning.
4. **Editorial reliability model:** every guide shows verified date + next review date + official source list; informational-only disclaimer.
5. **Planning logic:** pick route → download official checklist → map each China-issued document to apostille/legalisation/notarisation/translation → check validity windows → unify name spelling → keep one master file.
6. **Risk awareness baked in:** DNV renewal risk, tax-residence cautions, investor route "careful citizenship analysis" and ended programmes are flagged rather than upsold.

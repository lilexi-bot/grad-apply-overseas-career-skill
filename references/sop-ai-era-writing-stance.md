# 26fall AI-Era SOP Writing Stance — use AI, own it, de-GPT the output

> Field stance note (Chinese original verbatim at the bottom). Load when: the user is anxious about
> using AI to draft an SOP, or when a programme explicitly flags AI use. This file articulates the
> repo's position: AI-assisted drafting is the 26fall-era norm, and institutions that reject for
> AI use are self-selecting against exactly the cohort they want. Also provides a reusable prompt
> template for producing programme-tailored SOPs.
> （AI写文书， 学术不端， vibe coding， 斯坦福， 耶鲁 AI declaration， gpt味儿， 26fall， prompt）。

## The stance
1. **AI-assisted drafting is the 26fall-era norm.** Stanford now offers a vibe-coding course that
   encourages students to write zero lines of code by hand; Yale's application portal has an
   explicit "AI declaration" field asking applicants to describe how they used AI in their
   application — which is the institutional equivalent of *defaulting to AI-assisted applications*.
2. **If a school rejects you for using AI, that is data about the school, not about you.**
   Admissions officers who issue rejection letters on the basis of AI use are selecting for a
   cohort that does not embrace AI. A school's strength and atmosphere are driven by its students,
   not its masters or its buildings; if the students are not AI-era natives, you don't want to go
   there.
3. **In practice, admissions officers do not have time to scrutinise every SOP.** The effective
   bar is: tell your own story, show distinctive traits, match the programme concretely,
   **de-GPT the language**, and you will be fine.

## The reusable prompt template
The author's own prompt for producing a programme-tailored SOP:

> 我正在申请 **[xx 学校]** 的 **[xx program]**，现在我要写 SOP。我给你的参考信息有：
> **[xx 学校 xx program 的课程]**，**[我的简历]**，**[我申请其他学校 xx 专业相关项目的 SOP]**，
> 这些基本能构成所有要求的内容，你只需要在表述上适当修改以满足 **[xx 学校 xx program]** 的
> SOP 的 instructions 即可。我的 primary research interest 是 **[xxxx]**。
> 接下来你先整理 SOP 要求，然后规划一下行文，我们先对齐一下文书的要点，先不成文。

### Why this prompt works
- **Three-source triangulation:** programme curriculum + your CV + your prior SOP for a related
  programme → guarantees content coverage.
- **Programme-specific instruction compliance:** the model adapts phrasing to the specific
  programme's instructions, not to invent from scratch.
- **"先不成文" (don't write the essay yet):** force an alignment step before drafting. This is the
  single highest-leverage move — it prevents a full-paragraph rewrite later.

## Practical "de-GPT" checklist
Before submitting any AI-drafted SOP, run through:
1. Replace generic superlatives ("incredibly passionate", "deeply committed") with specific moments.
2. Replace balanced, symmetrical paragraphs with *asymmetrical* ones — real narratives have uneven weights.
3. Add at least one *concrete proper noun* per paragraph (a professor's name, a paper title, a lab, a specific course code).
4. Read aloud — if any sentence sounds like a LinkedIn post, rewrite it.
5. Confirm every claim is traceable to your actual experience.

## The regional split in one line

| Region | Admissions officer's core question | Center of gravity |
|---|---|---|
| 🇬🇧 UK | "Why can you study this programme well?" | **Academic persuasion** |
| 🇭🇰 HK | "What kind of talent will you become after this programme?" | **Career persuasion** |
| 🇸🇬 SG | "Do you have the potential to be a leader in this field and integrate into SG / Asian industry?" | **Hybrid: academic + career + regional ecosystem** |

## 🇬🇧 UK PS — academic persuasion

### Core questions
- Do you genuinely love this discipline?
- Is your academic foundation strong enough?
- Do you have critical-thinking ability?

### Writing emphasis
- Roughly **half the PS** devoted to academic interest + critical reflection.
- LSE-style: deeply bind academic experiences to past practice — almost entirely academic-first.

### Canonical structure
1. 兴趣来源 → 2. 学术准备 → 3. 理论实践 → 4. 职业目标 → 5. Why School → 6. Conclusion

## 🇭🇰 HK PS — career persuasion

### Core questions
- Is your career goal clear?
- Do you understand the HK job market?
- How does this programme fit your career plan?

### Writing emphasis
- Career planning occupies **30–40% of the PS**.
- Emphasis on **Why Hong Kong** and **Why this programme**, not "why this school academically".

### Canonical structure
1. 动机原点 → 2. 能力证明 → 3. 项目理解与职业规划 (core) → 4. 贡献与结尾

## 🇸🇬 SG PS — hybrid

### Core questions
- Do you have the potential to be a leader in this field?
- Can you integrate into Singapore / Asian industry ecosystems?

### Writing emphasis
- Values: sensitivity to frontier trends + cross-disciplinary problem-solving.
- Must demonstrate awareness of the field's disruptive challenges in Asia / globally.
- Must articulate the *strategic reasoning* for choosing Singapore as a career launchpad.

### Canonical structure
1. 前沿痛点洞察 → 2. 跨学科学术/实践储备 → 3. 清晰的职业锚点与产业贡献 → 4. 为何是新加坡/该项目 → 5. 未来愿景与回馈

## 中文原稿（verbatim）

🇬🇧英国的文书注重学术说服，招生官的核心问题是"为什么你可以学好这个专业？"。 我们需要回答，你对这个学科是不是真的热爱？你的学术基础够吗？你有没有critical thinking能力？一篇好的英国PS通常一半左右的篇幅都在讲学术兴趣和鸭牛思考。像LSE，要求把学术经历和过往经验深度绑定，几乎是学术优先的思路。 ✔️UK PS典型结构是：兴趣来源→学术准备→理论实践→职业目标→Why School→Conclusion 🇭🇰香港文书注重职业说服，招生官核心问题是"你读完这个项目可以成为什么类型的人才？"。 我们要回答"你的职业目标是否清晰？"、"你对香港就业市场是否了解？"、"这个项目对你职业规划有什么帮助？"；职业规划通常占香港PS中30%-40%的篇幅。港校更强调Why Hong Kong，Why this program，而非学术上为什么选择这所学校。 ✔️HK PS 典型结构是：动机原点→能力证明→项目理解与职业规划→贡献与结尾，整篇文书的核心都在职业这块 🇸🇬新加坡是这两种的结合，招生官的问题是"你是否具备潜力成为该领域的领导者？能哦服融入新加坡和亚洲的产业生态"，新加坡非常看重个人对行业前沿趋势的敏锐度和跨学科解决复杂问题的能力。 我们需要回答"你是否看到xx领域在亚洲/全球即将面临的颠覆性挑战？"、"你选择新加坡作为职业起点的战略考量是什么？" ✔️SG PS 典型结构：前沿痛点洞察 → 跨学科学术/实践储备 → 清晰的职业锚点与产业贡献 → 为何是新加坡/该项目（课程/教授/实验室资源） → 未来愿景与回馈。

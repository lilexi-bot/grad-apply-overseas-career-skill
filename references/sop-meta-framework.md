## The three questions every admissions committee is really asking

### Q1 — Why do you want to study this field?
> 你为什么想读这个专业
What the committee is actually testing: **Do you have sufficient motivation?**
- Not "I've always liked X since childhood" — sentiment is not motivation.
- Motivation must be traceable to a *specific sequence of experiences* and a *specific unresolved
  question* that only graduate study can answer.

### Q2 — Why do you want to study it in *this* specific programme?
> 你为什么想在这个特定的 Program 读
What the committee is actually testing: **Do you have a goal and a plan, and do you know how to
use the programme's resources well?**
- Not "your ranking / your location / your reputation" — flattery, not a plan.
- A real plan names: specific faculty (and why them, concretely), specific labs / courses /
  resources (and why they fit your unresolved question), specific downstream path the programme
  enables.

### Q3 — What advantages do you bring?
> 你有什么优势
What the committee is actually testing: **Do you have the ability to achieve your goal?**
- Not a CV recitation — that is a list, not an argument.
- An advantage must be *framed* as: why *this* background makes *me* specifically suited to answer
  *that* question, in *this* programme, better than other candidates with similar CVs.

## The underlying competency triad

| What the question asks | What the committee is grading |
|---|---|
| Q1 — Why this field | 动力是否充足 |
| Q2 — Why this programme | 是否有目标与计划，是否知道如何利用资源 |
| Q3 — What are your advantages | 是否有能力实现目标 |

## The ten-beat writing scaffold
Every paragraph can be reduced to answering one of these ten beats:

1. **我是谁** — Who am I (identity, not CV bullet)
2. **我是一个什么样的人** — What kind of person am I (character / working style)
3. **我过去做得好还是不好** — What did I do well or poorly
4. **为什么做好了** — Why did I do well (attribution of success)
5. **为什么没有做好** — Why did I not do well (attribution of failure)
6. **我将来想做什么** — What do I want to do going forward
7. **为什么想做这个** — Why this (specific, not vague)
8. **为什么我认为我有可能做成这件事** — Why do I believe I can do it
9. **为什么我需要一个研究生/博士学位** — Why do I need graduate study at all
10. **为什么贵校/贵项目是我的一个非常好的选项** — Why *this* programme

Beats 1-5 answer Q3 (what do you bring). Beats 6-7 answer Q1 (why this field). Beats 8-10 answer
Q2 (why this programme). The SOP is complete when every beat has been concretely addressed.

## Anti-patterns
- **CV-in-prose.** Beating 1-5 as chronological CV rewrite — the SOP is an *argument*.
- **Generic flattery.** Beating 10 as "your programme is prestigious" — must name specific faculty / labs / courses.
- **Sentiment-as-motivation.** Beating 6-7 as "I've always loved X since childhood" — must trace to a specific unresolved question.
- **Hedging narrative.** Beat 8 as "I have this plan but also a backup plan" — committees read hedging as low-conviction.

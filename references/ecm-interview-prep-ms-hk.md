## Key Takeaways (English)

1. **Placement Types — must-know distinctions for ECM interviews.**
   | Type | Description |
   | --- | --- |
   | **Primary Placement** | New shares issued by the company; proceeds go to the issuer; dilutes existing shareholders |
   | **Secondary Placement** | Existing shareholders sell their shares; proceeds go to the selling shareholders; no dilution |
   | **Top-up Placement** | Hybrid — issuer issues new shares to top up a secondary sale so total shares sold match demand; combines primary + secondary mechanics |

2. **Block Trade vs. Normal Placement — Settlement difference.** Block trades settle faster (typically T+2 or even same-day/T+1 for accelerated bookbuild) because they involve selling existing shares in a large block, usually at a discount to market price, with minimal regulatory filings. A new share Primary Placement involves longer settlement (typically T+7 or more in HK) because of regulatory approval, prospectus/filing requirements, and the mechanics of issuing new shares.

3. **"Why Move" — the safe narrative framework for lateral hires.**
   - ❌ Never badmouth the current/former employer
   - ❌ Never say "I didn't get enough deals" (signals you're passive or unsuccessful)
   - ✅ Correct framing: "My current platform's strategic focus has shifted toward IPO products, and I'm looking for more exposure to Placement and Sponsor Deals to broaden my ECM product experience."
   - Key principle: frame the move as **pull toward growth**, not **push away from problems**.

4. **Deal Experience — structure your storytelling.**
   The interviewer cares less about how impressive the deal is, and more about **what YOU personally did**. Recommended structure:
   - **Step 1 — Deal Background & Fundamental**: Briefly set the scene (issuer, deal type, size, market conditions)
   - **Step 2 — Your specific role**: Walk through your contribution in each phase:
     - **Due Diligence**: What did you review? What issues did you flag? How did you coordinate with legal/accountants?
     - **Marketing**: How did you contribute to investor targeting, roadshow materials, anchor investor outreach?
     - **Settlement/Execution**: What was your role in pricing, allocation, closing logistics?
   - Key principle: show **ownership and granularity**, not just deal name-dropping.

5. **Strategic implication for ECM candidates.** The three mentoring points map to the three core dimensions ECM interviewers evaluate:
   - **Technical competence** → Can you explain how equity offerings work?
   - **Motivation & fit** → Are you moving toward something real, or running away from problems?
   - **Hands-on experience** → Did you actually do the work, or just watch from the sideline?
   
   Prepare for each dimension with specific, structured examples. Quality of self-reflection beats quantity of deals touched.

# Leadership Narrative Ladder — from event organiser to system builder

> Field framework from real master's/MBA essay coaching (Chinese original verbatim at the bottom).
> Load when: drafting leadership paragraphs for master's/MBA/SOP essays, mining the Evidence Map for
> leadership stories, planning extracurriculars, or planning promotion impact at work
> （领导力, leadership stories, MBA文书, 美硕文书, 社团/mentor/legacy 素材， 晋升思路）.

## The ladder — each tier contains the previous one and adds a level

1. **Broad influence on a group （泛影响力）.** Ran a club event, N attendees; invited a company to host a
   speaker event and maintained the connection. *(Baseline for ordinary US master's applications.)*
2. **+ Strong individual influence （强影响力）.** Built an alumni-mentor ↔ mentee programme with
   *substantive* impact — mentees who actually changed their job-search path or landed offers because of
   it. *(Top US master's level.)*
3. **+ System building that outlives you (legacy).** Turned a one-off internship-company relationship into
   a standing recruiting channel, lowering entry barriers for people with no experience; or set up
   local-company consulting projects that kept running after graduation and were later replicated by
   others. *(DBA/practice-doctorate level: broad + strong influence + a system that remains after you leave.)*
4. **Point-fix → pattern-fix.** Reviewing an analyst's work: find an error → leave a comment (point fix);
   notice the same class of error recurring → call people in, explain WHY this class keeps happening
   (pattern fix) — but the audience is still only one level. *(Ordinary MBA baseline.)*
5. **One action, multiple audiences, organisational gap closed (Top MBA).** Call analyst1 AND associate1
   into the SAME meeting. To the analyst: why these errors recur and how to self-detect them. To the
   associate: demonstrate, through live questioning, how to think downstream — who touches this work next,
   who gets blocked, who must know today, how to re-sequence when things break — instead of handing over
   answers. Both junior levels in the room get exposure beyond their rank and see where their piece sits
   in the whole chain. **One move, different takeaway per person, gap filled — the practice persists after
   you leave.**

## How to use
- Place every existing leadership story on the ladder FIRST; upgrade the story's structure (what did you
  systematise? who else was affected? what outlived you?) before inventing new activities.
- Essay selection: top-programme essays need at least one tier-3/5 story; tier-1/2 items belong in CV
  bullets, not SOP paragraphs.
- Career/planning use (also valid for promotions): aim your next activity at the next rung — build the
  reusable channel, the recurring mechanism, the multi-level meeting — not the one-off event.
- Quantify every tier: attendees, mentee-to-offer outcomes, channel longevity, replication count.

## 中文原稿（verbatim）

这几天在写硕士申请/MBA的文书，大概讲一下怎么写leadership，也可以作为一些做活动/公司晋升思路：

普通美硕：
带领社团办活动、多少个人参加 → 请公司来做 speaker event、维护 connection
【个人领导力对群体的泛影响力】

Top 美硕：
做 mentor（校友学长学姐）- mentee 项目，有多少实质性的 impact — 几个学生因此改变求职路径、拿到 offer
【个人领导力对群体的泛影响力 + 个人强影响力】

DMBA：
和自己实习过的公司建立持续关系，把一次性的机会变成常规通道 → 降低没有经验的人进入的门槛；或者对接本地公司开设 consulting project，并且让它在自己毕业之后还能继续跑下去 → 后来更多人开始复制同一套做法
【泛影响力 + 强影响力 + 系统搭建 — 离开之后还留得下的 legacy】

普通 MBA：
review analyst的工作，发现问题 → leave comment；发现同一类问题反复出现 → 把人叫来开会，讲清这类问题为什么会反复发生
【单点纠错 → 模式纠错，影响对象仍然只有一个人】

Top MBA：
同一场会，把analyst1 和asso1 两级一起叫进来
对analyst =这类问题为什么反复出现、怎么自己识别
对asso = 当场用一串追问示范怎么想（这批东西接下来到谁手上、谁会被卡、谁必须今天知道、出了问题后面怎么重排），而不是替他给答案
下级同时在场 = 拿到超出自己层级的 exposure，看见自己那一格的活在整条链里意味着什么
【一个动作同时对多类人生效，且每个人拿到的东西不一样 + 补上组织缺口 → 你走之后这套做法还在】

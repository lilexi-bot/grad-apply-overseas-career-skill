## English Key Takeaways

1. **Info gap is narrowing fast:** Silicon Valley–China information gap is now only ~2 weeks (vs. 3 months for NYC–SF). The value of being in SV is now about *felt experience* (体感) — sensing the intensity of emotions and actual market heat, not learning new facts. Embodied AI startups are springing up like "bamboo shoots after rain."

2. **Neo Lab hangover:** Many VCs FOMO'd into a wave of "Neo Lab" startups at the start of the year; few have delivered results yet. The music hasn't stopped, but sentiment is slightly improved.

3. **FOMO still dominant:** Jev and Instinct raising $10B and still being oversubscribed shows FOMO is alive and well.

4. **Application layer vs. model layer ROI question:** Total application-layer ARR / application-layer investment is far below the same ratio for model layer. Two interpretations: (a) it hasn't arrived yet (timing), or (b) the model itself IS the application (structural). This is an open question.

5. **Bubble consensus, timing disagreement:** Projects starting at ~$100M valuation, hitting $1-3B within months for round 2, still oversubscribed. Everyone agrees there's a bubble and a correction should come — but probability estimates for correction within 6-12 months range wildly from 20% to 80%.

6. **AMD acquires World Lab — positioning startup exit strategy:** The key to getting acquired is finding the narrative pain point the buyer is missing. AMD needed a specific story; World Lab filled it. This is the "positioning startup" playbook.

7. **SV VC focus areas align with China:** Robotics, new chips, new infra, AI4S — similar thematic focus. But the cognitive framing differs.

8. **Open-source pre-train + post-train framework architecture:** TML (product-oriented) and Fireworks (customization-oriented) represent the two ends of the spectrum. In between, a large cohort of RL/post-train-as-service companies. Debate: some see Qiao Lin as the killer; others worry about margins and bet TML wins.

9. **RSI in 3-6 months → researcher displacement:** AI researchers will need new outlets. Robotics and AI4S are the perceived destinations, but whether they can absorb this many researchers is uncertain.

10. **OAI pivoting to robotics training data:** Currently at ~1% of robotics data; the question is whether it could reach 90% in 6 months — a potential inflection point.

# Hard-Tech Founder Fundraising Ecosystem (strategy reference)

> Context: this skill's founder track assumes a HARD-TECH venture raising venture capital globally,
> not a lifestyle business. Direction: overseas — Silicon Valley / YC / California / Middle East
> sovereign capital; domestic China — 红杉/硬科技基金/PE. Not investment advice; verify current terms.

## Why fundraising strategy = visa strategy
- **US IER parole** effectively outsources screening to investors: you need $311,071 from *qualified US
  investors* (or $124,429 government grants) within 18 months, ≥10% ownership. "Qualified investor"
  track-record benchmark ~$746,571 in prior startup investments (rule threshold; verify on uscis.gov/i-941).
  → A YC SAFE can count; a friends-and-family round usually does not.
- **Singapore EntrePass** explicitly credits recognised accelerators (Y Combinator, MassChallenge listed)
  or SGD100k single-round funding from recognised VCs.
- **UK Innovator Founder** and **French Tech Visa** test innovation/viability/scalability — accelerator
  acceptance and traction letters materially strengthen endorsement.

  Techstars, Berkeley SkyDeck, StartX (Stanford-affiliated), Plug and Play, Alchemist (hard-tech/enterprise),
  Founders Inc., HF0.
- **Hard-tech / deep-tech VCs:** Khosla Ventures, Lux Capital, DCVC, Founders Fund, Breakthrough Energy
  Ventures (climate/energy), The Engine (MIT-spun, tough-tech), Playground Global (hardware),
  a16z, Sequoia (US/EU entity post-2023 split), Lightspeed, Benchmark, NEA.
- **Non-dilutive / government:** NSF SBIR/STTR (up to ~$1.25M+ across phases; counts toward IER grant
  track), DOE/ARPA-E grants, DIU & AFWERX (defense commercialisation), CHIPS Act funding for semiconductors.

## Overseas — Middle East (sovereign capital & deep-tech build-out)

  MBZUAI (free AI master's → research→founder pipeline), G42 ecosystem; Mubadala / ADQ venture arms.
- **Saudi Arabia:** KAUST (free master's + startup grants), SDAIA, LEAP conference, Aramco Wa'ed Ventures,
  PIF / Sanabil Investments (soverean VC allocator), NEOM tech ecosystem.

  founders often incorporate a US C-Corp while building research/pilot partnerships in the Gulf.

## Output rule for this skill
When generating `/founder-deck` or investor strategy, ALWAYS:
1. Print the official visa source link next to any visa threshold cited.
2. Mark investor/accelerator names as "to be re-verified for current terms".
3. Recommend US C-Corp (Delaware) vs China-entity vs dual-structure discussion with a startup lawyer — never assert a structure.
4. Never claim a warm intro, funding likelihood, or visa approval probability as fact.

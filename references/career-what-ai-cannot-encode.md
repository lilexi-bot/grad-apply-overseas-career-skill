# What AI Cannot (Yet) Encode — human-native traits as temporary career moat

## The argument (reconstructed)
1. Human wisdom can be approximated by human language → therefore many believe "the boundary of
   language is the boundary of the world."
2. World change can be approximated by video → therefore many believe video models ARE world models.
3. The underlying mechanism in both cases: language and video can both be encoded as tokens.
4. Demis Hassabis (Google DeepMind founder) argues: everything that stably exists in the universe has
   a micro-structure (stock prices, protein folding, etc.). The world is therefore encodable, and
   therefore AI-learnable.
5. This sounds pessimistic long-term — nothing is beyond AI's eventual reach.
6. **But in the present:** while language is near-perfectly encoded, the world-as-entropy system cannot
   be perfectly encoded. **Human emotion, trust, taste, aesthetics, imagination cannot yet be
   perfectly encoded.**
7. People who possess these *human-native* traits are therefore temporarily irreplaceable.

## The framework
| Layer | Encodable? | What's approximated | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Language | ✅ yes, near-perfect | Human wisdom (biased) | Done |
| Video / pixels | ✅ yes, rapidly improving | World change | In progress |
| Stock prices / protein folding | ✅ yes (Hassabis's thesis) | Stable micro-structures | In progress |
| Emotion | ⚠️ partial approximation | Human inner state | Not yet |
| Trust | ❌ not tokenisable | Relational state between humans | Human-native |
| Taste / aesthetics | ❌ not tokenisable | Preference + judgement signal | Human-native |
| Imagination | ❌ not tokenisable | Novel combinatorial leaps | Human-native |

## What this means for career investment
- **Bet against the tokenisable.** Skills that look like "I can encode X into a language my audience
  understands" are the first to be commoditised by AI. Examples: summarisation, template CV writing,
  boilerplate code, generic analysis.
- **Bet on the human-native.** Trust, taste, aesthetics, imagination, emotion — these are NOT skills
  you "learn" like a framework; they are cultivated over years of living, reading, and making
  judgement calls. They are the moat.
- This is NOT a Luddite position. It is a framing for *where to place the next bet* in your own
  development. Even if long-term AI catches everything, the human-native moat buys you a decade+ of
  differentiated value.
- Connects to the mindset file (`career-mindset-adaptability.md`): the meta-skill of "responding to
  world change" is itself partly human-native — it requires taste to know *what* has changed, not
  just that change happened.
- Connects to the interview notes (`interview-communication-notes.md`): presence in S&T /
  front-office roles, the founder's "adaptive vs pushback" instinct, the 60-second
  explanation-to-layperson ability — all draw on trust + taste, not just information encoding.
- Connects to the leadership ladder (`leadership-ladder.md`): the top tier (one action, multiple
  audiences, organisational gap closed) requires reading people in the room — a fundamentally
  non-tokenisable skill.

## The optimistic reading
Long-term pessimism ("AI will learn everything") collapses into short-term optimism ("what it
cannot encode yet is where your next 10 years of edge lives"). The pessimist / optimist framing
flips once you name the boundary correctly.

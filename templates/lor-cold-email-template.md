# LOR Outlines & Professor Cold-Email Templates

## Recommendation letter outline (give this to your referee)
1. Relationship: how long, in what capacity (course grade rank, research supervision), comparison cohort.
2. Two specific anecdotes showing ability (one technical, one maturity/collaboration).
3. Quantified benchmark vs peers ("top 5% of students I have taught in N years").
4. Explicit recommendation strength + contact for verification.
> Remind referees: letters must be their own; provide the outline and your evidence map, never a ghostwritten final.

## Cold email to professor (research / pre-doc)
Subject: Prospective [PhD/MS+research] applicant — [your topic] — [your name]

Dear Prof. [Name],
I am applying to [programme] for 2027 entry. Your work on [SPECIFIC paper/result, correctly cited] resonates
with my project [1-line + quantified result + repo/paper link]. I would be grateful for 15 minutes to ask
whether your group plans to take students in [area]. I understand you are busy; a one-line reply suffices.
Best regards, [name] + CV link

## Rules
- Send only after reading ≥2 recent papers; never mass-email the same text.
- Keep under 180 words; attach a 1-page CV, not a transcript.

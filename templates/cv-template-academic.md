# CV Template — Academic / Research (English, fill-in)

> Structured after a reviewed Word CV template (donor-provided, 2026-09). Optimised for research-track
> master's / PhD applications and research/RA jobs. For industry/internship CVs use `cv-template.md`
> (which carries the revision rules R1–R3). Fill every bracket; delete sections you don't have rather
> than leaving placeholders. Quantify everything. One page for master's applicants, two acceptable for
> PhD with strong research.

## Section logic (why this order)
- Education → Achievements → Publications → Patents FIRST: research readers screen on scholarly signal
  before reading experience.
- Skills bar lives LAST (unlike industry CVs) but R1 still applies: every claimed skill must be backed by
  a project/experience bullet — see `cv-template.md` revision rules.
- "Technical Projects" bullets follow the Evidence Map pattern: problem → your action → quantified
  result → artifact.
- Keep research interests faculty-readable and SPECIFIC (match to target professor papers, not buzzwords).

## Track emphasis cheatsheet
- **fineng:** stochastic calculus, numerical computing, backtesting, PnL/risk metrics, C++/Python
- **cs / ds:** models shipped, scale (QPS/latency/data size), LLM/agent/RL pipelines
- **ece / mech:** hardware platforms, sim tools (Isaac Sim etc.), PCB/FPGA/robot stack
- **techtransfer:** cross-industry communication, commercialisation, IP, market sizing
- **founder:** traction first — users/revenue/funding/IP/press; founder-market fit in one line

## CV revision rules (from real resume reviews — enforced during `/cv-review`)

### R1. The Skills bar must be backed by the body
- Every technology in the Skills section that you genuinely know MUST also appear in a project/experience
  bullet where you actually used it. If several projects used the same technology, demonstrate it in full
  in ONE place only — no repeated shallow mentions across entries.
- Technologies you only know lightly (no project to show) may stay in the Skills bar — but they become a
  **pre-interview study list**: learn them well enough to be grilled on before the interview.
- Rule of thumb: a skill with zero supporting bullet → either remove it or be ready to defend it.

### R2. Experience hires (1–3 yrs full-time): make it LOOK different from a student CV
- Visual weight: the full-time experience section must read clearly heavier than internships — at least
  ~2x the bullet count/length of an average internship entry.
- Structure: group by major project/programme, OR merge several small projects into one entry emphasizing
  aggregate impact. Never present post-graduation work as internship-sized fragments.
- Lead with scope/ownership (systems owned, decisions made, people coordinated), not task execution.

### R3. Experience hires (3–5 yrs): balance internal leadership vs external/client-facing
- A senior-ish CV needs BOTH sides: (a) internal — team/tech leadership, mentoring, architecture,
  cross-team coordination; (b) external — clients/customers/stakeholders, requirement negotiation,
  delivery, incident communication.
- Allocate bullets roughly evenly; technical depth must show on both sides, not only in internal work.
- Founder variant: external = fundraising/clients/pipeline metrics; internal = hiring/architecture/IP.

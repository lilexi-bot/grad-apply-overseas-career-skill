# SOP / Personal Statement Outline

> Narrative rule: admissions officers test (1) why this field, (2) why you, (3) why this programme.
> Every claim must trace to the Evidence Map. Structure your unique story yourself — AI polishes, it does not invent.

1. **Hook (1 paragraph):** the specific problem / moment that pulled you into this field. Concrete, not generic.
2. **Foundation:** coursework + 2–3 evidence entries showing depth and progression (technical detail appropriate to track).
3. **Pivot / depth project:** your strongest project — problem, your decision, result, what you realized you still lack.
4. **Why now / why this degree:** the explicit capability gap this master's fills (name courses, faculty, labs from official pages).
5. **Why this programme:** 3 programme-specific reasons (curriculum, lab, ecosystem / founder resources), not rankings.
6. **Future goals:** short-term (role / type of firm / venture) and long-term; for founder track: venture thesis + jurisdiction logic.
7. **Close:** one sentence tying back to the hook.

## Track reorientation notes
- **fineng:** mathematical modelling + market-practice awareness; mention a specific market phenomenon you studied.
- **cs/ds:** system depth and scale metrics; avoid buzzword stacking.
- **techtransfer:** R&D → commercialisation arc; LP-vs-builder industry observation.
- **interdisciplinary:** engineering × AI × art/capital cross-domain story, still evidence-based.
- **founder:** founder-market fit; traction over claims; why this programme AND this jurisdiction (capital, customers, visa).

## Review checklist (admissions-officer mode scores these)
- [ ] Every paragraph contains evidence, not adjectives
- [ ] No cliché ("since childhood", "world-renowned", "deeply fascinated")
- [ ] Programme-specific names verified on official pages
- [ ] Word count within limit; technical depth matches track
- [ ] Final version human-rewritten + native proofread before submission

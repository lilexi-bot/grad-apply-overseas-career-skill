## Entry template (copy per item)

### E01 — [Project / event name]
- **Timeline:** YYYY.MM – YYYY.MM · role / affiliation
- **Type:** research / hackathon / open-source / internship / startup / competition / publication
- **Problem & context:** one paragraph, what bottleneck existed
- **Technical stack & methodology:** languages, frameworks, hardware, algorithms, math
- **What YOU did:** your specific contribution (not the team's)
- **Quantified results:** metrics with baseline → result (e.g. latency 10.8ms → 4.1ms, -62%; QPS ×2.58)
- **Artifacts / proof:** repo URL, paper, demo, award certificate, star count, user numbers
- **Reflection & linkage:** insight gained; how it motivates the target programme / venture
- **Reusable tags:** #fineng #cs #founder #systems ...
